Good to Know

Common questions before you contract.

Practical answers for property owners comparing maintenance, renovation, and commercial fitout partners.

What specific services does The Fitout Team provide?
We provide high-end commercial fitouts, luxury residential renovations, and specialized technical maintenance (including AC, electrical, plumbing, and AMC packages) across Dubai.
Do you handle necessary municipality approvals?
Yes. Our engineering teams assist with drafting, submitting, and acquiring necessary permits and approvals from Dubai Municipality, Civil Defence, and building management.
Can I book maintenance and renovation together?
Absolutely. Post-handover, many clients opt into our Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) to ensure their newly installed MEP systems and finishes remain in pristine condition.
